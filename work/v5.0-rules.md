# SMCITY 5.0

Backup: `backup-v4.6-2026-10-31`, commit `44e4ba020a49c64e45353e407d92c1d081b424d9`.
Implementation branch: `version-5.0`. This branch contains the 5.0 release implementation. The 4.6 backup is retained independently.

## Rules

- Result/statistical day: 06:00–06:00. Calendar-based shifts and activities continue across the boundary.
- Main toolbar: **ภารกิจ : …%**. Official city score uses the most recently closed round. Until a completed round has results, the city starts at 100% without retroactive penalties.
- Every decided trip has equal weight: on time 1; late, missed, unavailable, cancelled 0. Staying in the same building does not create another trip or score.
- Itineraries contain at least four planned destinations, with staggered routines, reasonable activity stays and fixed appointments including return home. Activities use the real arrival location; moving vehicles finish their current trip before advancing the itinerary.
- Missing, full or disconnected service: replace the intended activity with an actual routed report to the civic center. An already-reserved destination reached late fails instead of becoming a report.
- Being already inside the civic center permits filing another issue without fabricating another successful trip. Reports preserve the actual cause and affected home district.
- Each complaint gets 24 simulation hours from first notice; further reporters do not restart its clock. Distance problems notify the player without replacing an existing reservation.
- Unresolved overdue issues reduce following-day gross income to 50%; failed report trips reduce it to 0%. Maximum penalty once per round; full maintenance. Regular taxes/business, tourist fees and incident rewards obey the factor. The existing logo money cheat remains a cheat.
- Immigration: official score >=70%. Tourism: >=80%, subject to existing capacity limits. At 06:00, residents below 40% for decided personal trips prepare to drive out. Empty result days do not cause eviction. Existing three-day housing cooldown applies on actual departure.
- Homes/hotels share the housing category. Casinos join leisure. New citizen-service category contains an instanced five-wing government building, with an open courtyard, night facade and low-quality model.
- No extra first-build grace period was added: that suggestion was not confirmed. The complaint grace remains exactly 24 hours; inability to file a report uses the agreed 0% rule.

## Verification and known limits

- Publishing retains the existing building, parking, lane-safety and graphics gates and now also requires the 5.0 mission-rules job.
- Maintained checks cover equal trip scoring, the 06:00 boundary, complaints and income factors, 40% emigration, active trips, repeated save/reload, green-light release and flying traffic. Civic geometry is checked at all four rotations and three quality levels.
- 350 seeded parking-admission scenarios match the previous swept collision decisions, including blocked and allowed exits. Curve samples fell from 450,265 to 35,468 (92.1%). Cached geometry also matches the old curve numerically, including in-place coordinate edits and appended points.
- A 120-frame headless London comparison (1,000 residents, 479 active cars) took 47.24 seconds on 4.6 and 5.39 seconds with the new parking optimizations. This is one CPU stress case, not a general FPS claim or a guarantee for physical iPads. Static daily travel estimates are shared separately from live routing and capacity checks.
- Chromium smoke checks use actual WebGL, build the civic center, check runtime errors and capture desktop/day plus portrait/night views. This does not replace a physical iPad/Safari test.
- The accelerated one-day stress run for 100/500/1,000 residents checks duplicate vehicles, parking capacity and saves. The unchanged London layout has poor mission results at very large time steps and needs player balance testing; do not interpret a successful stress-test exit as a balanced city.
- Some historical fixtures still assert superseded 4.6-era house dimensions, old City Data/report placement or previous popup timing. Their failures remain visible in the existing non-publishing `test` job; do not claim the full historical suite passes.

## Compatibility

Legacy happiness fields and DOM/save keys remain inert compatibility aliases. They no longer determine growth, emigration or gameplay rewards. Old saves retain their vehicles, live reservations, current shifts and itineraries. Future unused calendars receive the new rules; old happiness history is not converted into failed trips.

## Compact civic buildings

- New civic choices: 3x3 with five parking spaces or compact 8x8 with twenty spaces. The latter is sized by the existing minimum-area bank selector.
- Pentagon wings and entry are contained in the building zone; the frontage traffic aisle is kept open, and both use the shared parking-line renderer.
- The former sixty-space civic tier is retired for construction but retained when loading old saves, preserving parked vehicles and plots.
