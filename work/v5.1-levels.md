# SMCITY 5.1 — City level, prize crates and AI junction credits

The pre-upgrade 5.0 main commit is preserved on branch `backup/smcity-v5.0-before-level-rewards-20261101` (commit `c9eb44cafc091f6d088f7ff7d3a74c1557511b6b`).

## City progression
- Each city save tracks `cityLevel.peakPopulation`, the maximum **resident** population ever observed. Tourists do not count. Emigration never reduces the peak or unlock progress.
- The first milestone is 20 residents; later milestones are at 70, 120, 170, ... (50 more residents per level). Cities begin at Level 1. Example: if the peak was 100 and the city shrinks to 30, the next threshold is still 120.
- Progress is computed in the existing UI update pass using the already-computed population, not by adding a new per-frame resident scan.
- The HUD shows the current level, peak population, next target, remaining residents to the next level, pending crates and AI credit balance.

## Rewards
Each attained milestone creates **one pending crate**, selecting one of three outcomes with equal probability at unlock; the selected outcome is persisted and not re-rolled by reopening the popup. A crate is claimed only when the player opens it:
1. 1,000,000 city money.
2. Four F1 vehicle technology entitlements: one speed, one smart, one flying, one teleport. Entitlements are applied through existing vehicle technology quotas to distinct eligible residents; unavailable car assignments remain in those quotas for future residents.
3. One AI intersection upgrade credit (equal to one free installation of the existing AI traffic light). Paid installation for 1,000,000 remains available. Each free installation spends exactly one credit; purchased junctions cannot be bought twice. Demolition does not refund either payment type.

## Saves and compatibility
- New state is included in the existing format-2 city snapshot as `cityLevel`: version 51, population peak, level, pending reward objects, claimed count, remaining AI credits and bounded reward history.
- Saved reward entitlements and credit balances survive city save/load. A fresh city resets level progress.
- **Older saves have no historical highest-population record.** On first load into 5.1, progress is initialized from the **currently observable resident population**. It cannot reconstruct a previous peak that fell before version 5.1. Existing money, buildings, residents, vehicles and AI signals are preserved.
- No change to core traffic routing, Performance scoring, resident scheduling or money formulas.
