# SMCITY 5.3 — resident mission rules

## Rules
- A new resident work/service/home trip has exactly 180 game minutes from creation (including its departure queue) to the building entrance. Arrival at exactly 180 minutes is on time.
- Service success is recorded only after the complete service duration, starting when parking finishes. Closed venues, full service capacity, insufficient remaining opening hours, cancellation, and late entrance are distinct failures.
- Work must still be reached before the shift ends. Spacing a lunch routine must retain the full shift.
- Both daily and adaptive plans keep starts at least 180 minutes apart. Actual starts also respect this interval; a delayed previous activity does not consume the next trip's allowance.
- Missing buildings/routes/capacity remain real city problems. A delayed itinerary alone no longer creates the misleading pre-departure “เวลาในตารางไม่พอ” failure.
- Scores and bonuses are idempotent. A service in progress across 06:00 is settled on completion. Parking after an on-time entrance is not retroactively marked late at rollover.

## Fixes
1. Replace estimated-route-plus-15 scoring deadlines with the fixed 180-minute resident allowance.
2. Recalculate the allowance when the actual destination is selected; preserve the actual destination in diagnostics.
3. Retain known work/home destinations and planned deadlines in unstarted-result reports.
4. Preserve the afternoon end of a work shift when lunch/resume steps are collapsed by spacing.
5. Allow an early shift's departure to fall before calendar midnight.
6. Apply spacing to adaptive outings and actual starts.
7. Defer successful service scoring until full service, after parking; cancel unfinished service on demolition, early departure, removal, or manual day skip.
8. Keep retry timestamps authoritative over copied event metadata (prevents an overdue-event loop).
9. Distinguish capacity, parking, closure, shift end and travel-limit failures; do not call a late result successful in reports.

## Validation
- 21 dedicated mission-rule cases; 700 generated plans including 85 lunch-style work plans.
- Existing rule, travel, lifestyle, mission-dashboard, tourism, lane-change, deadlock, turn-clearance and flight-layer tests run separately.
- Event-level itinerary integration uses real route/reservation/arrival functions with controlled 10-minute travel; 157 arrivals, 43 after 20:00 and 15 after midnight in the recorded run.
- Browser visual verification was blocked by the desktop browser's local-file URL policy; no visual pass is claimed.
- Standalone packaging uses the repository's build-release.cjs with an in-memory filesystem writer because local shell writes failed. Its generated files are materialized through the workspace file tool and verified against the tested game source.
